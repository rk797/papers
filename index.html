    Ryan Khoshkbarchi, Rishabh Agarwal, Jamie MacDonald — University of Calgary

    A generative modeling approach using conditional GANs for synthesizing methane plume dispersion maps from environmental metadata, offering a cost effective alternative for stakeholders to interpret environmental impact from sparse or incomplete data sources as opposed to expensive satellite imaging.
